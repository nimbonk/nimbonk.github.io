GamePowerPlan

A Windows tray app that switches your power plan to a high performance plan while a game is running, then back to Balanced when you stop playing.

  • Detects games from the folders you choose, with no overlays or injection
  • Grace period, check interval, ignore list and optional global hotkey
  • No accounts, no analytics and no network access
